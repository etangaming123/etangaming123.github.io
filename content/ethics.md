# Ethics

## "Why on *Earth* would you need an Ethics page?"

Long story short, I've had a rough past. That rough past led me to saying things I shouldn't have said, done things I shouldn't have done, and so on. Specifics will not be named due to the severity, in some cases, and the cases being lost, somewhere on the internet, but definitely out there.

If you've seen my messages from years ago (not saying you should look for them), I have changed for the better, and am not the same person as the one who sent those messages or did those actions.

So yeah, these ethics are ones I (atleast try to) follow, just to be a better person. More ethics may be added, or existing ones may be tweaked depending on what I see fit. **This is not a full list**, there are way more ethics that I tend to follow, but these ones are the ones that I feel that need their own page.

Feel free to copy these for your own use cases!

## Be open and honest.

To a reasonable extent.

"Being open" means being open to all ideas, even to those you disagree. It doesn't mean you literally have to be open to all ideas - we all have disagreements, and that's okay! 

"Being honest" is self explanatory. While I do believe that *some things are best kept private*, there's no point in lying. After all, honesty is the best policy, and while it is tempting to make that small lie here and there, it builds the habit of turning those "small lies" into bigger ones. 

## Respect goes to most, if not all.

"Most, if not all" excludes the really **really bad** people. Examples will not be listed.

That aside, I strongly believe you should treat all with respect. A little kindness goes a long way, and never hurt anyone, did it?

This ethic also includes *not* offending others, whether by harassment or otherwise. Sure, it is tempting to talk back behind someone's back after they harmed you a lot, but are you going to let that stop you from being kind to all?

I personally struggle with meeting this ethic, and I know a lot of other people out there are too, but (as corny as it may sound) together we'll spread kindness around the world.

## Don't let your negative self drag others down.

It's okay to have bad days! There's those times when you feel like absolute trash, or other days when you want to let out your rage on something. But don't let that affect who you truely are, it's not okay to place unnecessary blame on other people.

This is an ethic I struggle with the most, as I tend to drag other people down for my own mistakes.

## Boundaries are everything.

Sure, poking fun here and there is okay, but you gotta ask yourself - "are they okay with this?"

And, if you feel like someone else is pushing your own boundaries, speak up! Whether it be a simple "hey dude I don't like that", or "YO STOP IT", you have power, and your boundaries should be respected, just like how you should respect others' own boundaries.

If I'm doing stuff you don't like, please do ask me to stop!

## Ask for help if needed!

If there's something you can't really do, or you're struggling a lot, there's help out there! Just remember to seek the most appropriate help. 

You're never alone on this. There's others out there, reach a hand!

*Admittedly, I tend - not - to follow this one. But you, the reader, should.*
